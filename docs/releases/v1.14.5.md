# Puntovivo v1.14.5 — clearer checkout and narrower desktop capabilities

## Why this release matters

This update makes everyday checkout cues easier to understand, improves small-
screen feedback, and removes a desktop database bridge that the application no
longer used. It also tightens how known identities are represented in Copilot
analytics requests. These are focused corrections to the existing POS, not a
new operating profile or a claim of production certification.

## What changed

### Checkout and operator feedback

- The mobile checkout bar keeps the full sale total visible instead of
  truncating it on narrow screens.
- Cash suggestions are labelled as amounts and ordered consistently. The exact-
  amount selection uses a half-cent tolerance; an amount a few cents short is
  no longer treated as exact. Suggestions are not presented as keyboard shortcuts.
- Toasts stay contained on narrow screens, remain right-aligned, and do not
  intercept clicks outside the notification itself.
- Site row actions have accessible names and tooltips. Voice availability can
  be checked by cashiers and refreshes after an administrator saves AI settings.

### Dates and recovery guidance

- Dashboard revenue labels preserve calendar dates rather than shifting them
  through timestamp conversion.
- Invalid or unsupported tenant timezones surface the existing repair guidance
  from date-bound commands. This does not change fiscal certification status.

### Privacy and desktop boundaries

- Copilot analytics uses request-local pseudonyms for identities known to its
  bounded snapshot, and applies known-value protection to conversation turns.
  This is data minimization, not a general detector of all personal information.
- The unused raw-database IPC bridge has been removed. The sandboxed renderer
  continues using the application API and its supported desktop capabilities;
  it does not receive direct database access.
- Startup assets and release-check coverage have been improved. These changes
  do not establish a new performance guarantee or qualify physical hardware.

## Before you use it

- These notes prepare the proposed release. They do not mean that v1.14.5 has
  been published or that an installer is available. The release decision and
  distribution checks remain separate from reviewing these changes.
- Production sale remains gated by fiscal certification and physical-hardware
  validation. See the
  [project status](https://github.com/johnny4young/puntovivo/blob/main/docs/PROJECT-STATUS.md)
  for the current capability and readiness boundaries.
- The privacy change does not remove AI-provider charges or make cancellation
  proof that no charge occurred.
