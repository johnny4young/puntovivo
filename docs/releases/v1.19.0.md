# Puntovivo v1.19.0 — A selling screen built around the ticket, and the manager's day on Hoy

## Why this release matters

A cashier spends the whole shift on one screen, and until now that screen split
attention between a scan field, a product search and a command palette, while
the total and the charge button moved as the ticket grew. v1.19.0 rebuilds the
selling screen around the ticket: the total and **Cobrar** stay in place, every
scan is confirmed with undo at hand, and cash can be charged in one touch with
the change already shown.

Managers get a single place to start the day. **Hoy** now opens on what sold,
what is pending before the close and how each register is doing. Stores that
sell by the case can finally scan a case by its own barcode.

## What changed

### Selling screen

- The ticket takes the main column as a table with line number, product, a
  quantity stepper, unit, price and total. The customer, the total, the tender
  keys and **Cobrar** are pinned on the right and keep their place while the
  ticket scrolls.
- A banner above the ticket confirms the last scan or add: product, price, stock
  left and **Undo**. It has a fixed slot, so the ticket never jumps.
- With cash selected, the screen offers exact cash and the next two round bills,
  each with the change it leaves. One touch opens the charge drawer with that
  amount ready to confirm. The tender is remembered per ticket.
- A strip above the ticket shows the register, cashier, site, open tickets with
  their totals, the price tier, and connection or device problems when there
  are any. A missing printer shows there, and managers can open printer setup
  from it.
- A line sold in a packaging unit shows the unit next to the product name, so a
  case of 12 never looks like a single unit.

### One bar for scan, search, quantity, customer and actions

- The scan field is now the register's single bar. Scanning and searching work
  as before.
- `3*` sets the quantity for the next scan, quick key or search pick. Decimals
  are accepted only for products sold by fraction.
- `@` searches active customers by name, email, phone or ID document and
  attaches one to the ticket. A suspended sale keeps it.
- `/` lists the register actions available right now, such as suspend, return,
  new sale, open drawer, charge or close register, with their keys.
- F5 still opens product search and Ctrl/Cmd+K still opens the app-wide palette.

### One-touch keys shared by the whole site

- The one-touch keys under the ticket now belong to the site, not to each
  browser. Every register of a site shows the same keys, up to eight, each with
  an optional name and colour.
- Managers and admins choose, name, colour, order and remove the keys from an
  editor opened from the strip.
- A key can sell by amount: the cashier types a value and the register adds the
  quantity it buys at the catalog price.
- The touch POS opens on a **Lo de siempre** tab with the same keys.
- A site without keys shows its top sellers of the week. The first register to
  open a site with no keys carries over its old local favourites, once.

### Hoy for managers

- Managers and admins see sales today against the same weekday last week, with
  an hour-by-hour curve, orders and average ticket.
- A pending list orders what blocks the close first: approvals waiting for a
  manager and registers left open since a previous day, then DIAN rejections
  and other failed deliveries. Each row has one action, such as approving with
  the staff PIN or counting and closing a register.
- Each open register shows its sales, its state and its next handoff from the
  schedule.
- Viewers keep the read-only summary, and the trends below stay for everyone.

### Products

- Each packaging presentation of a product has its own barcode field, and a save
  keeps it. Scanning that code sells the presentation at its own price and
  deducts its base units.
- Two presentations of the same product cannot share a code, and when old data
  has a repeated code the scanner now picks the same product every time.

### A new look and formal Spanish

- The app has a new visual identity: new colours in light and dark, new type,
  a navy navigation rail, flat buttons and a new logo and desktop icon. Orange
  is used only for **Cobrar**.
- The Spanish copy now addresses users with **usted** across the web app, the
  desktop app and printed receipts.

## Before you use it

- Cashiers will notice the new selling screen on the first sale. Show them the
  pinned total and **Cobrar**, the last-read banner with undo, the cash keys and
  the `3*`, `@` and `/` prefixes before their shift.
- One-touch keys chosen in a browser before this release move to the site the
  first time a register opens it. Review them in the editor afterwards.
- This release adds two database migrations, for the shared one-touch keys and
  for till incidents. They run on their own when the app starts.
- The server can record till incidents (stockouts, price mismatches, products
  without a barcode) and Hoy lists them, but the till has no screen to report
  them yet.
- The Colombian fiscal pack is still in test mode. Nothing is sent to DIAN.
- Windows builds are not code-signed yet, so a downloaded update waits for
  **Restart to install update** in the tray menu instead of installing on quit.
- Production sale is still gated by fiscal certification and physical-hardware
  validation, as the
  [project status](https://github.com/johnny4young/puntovivo/blob/main/docs/PROJECT-STATUS.md)
  describes.
