# Puntovivo v1.18.0 — DIAN numbering ranges and a desktop app that recovers on its own

## Why this release matters

Until now a Colombian store had no screen to register the numbering ranges that
DIAN authorizes. Without one, the electronic invoice request from v1.17.0 stayed
hidden, and nothing warned the owner before a range ran out or expired.
v1.18.0 adds that screen, picks the right range at each sale, and warns before
numbers or dates run out.

The desktop app also stops closing when its built-in server fails. The server
restarts on its own, the till shows that it is reconnecting, and the cashier is
asked to check the last sale. Backups no longer freeze the window while they
run.

## What changed

### DIAN numbering ranges

- **Company → Fiscal** has a **DIAN numbering ranges** card with one table per
  site. The form follows the order of the DIAN authorization, and the first
  number is confirmed before the range is saved.
- A range is registered, activated, retired or deleted, never edited, and every
  step is in the audit log. Each site, document type and environment has at
  most one active range.
- When the active range runs out or expires, the next sale closes it and moves
  to the range waiting behind it, in the same step. Two sales racing for the
  last number cannot both get it.
- Alerts warn 30 days before a range expires (critical at 15 business days,
  counted in Colombian time) and when 20% of its numbers are left (critical at
  5% or three days of sales). They appear on the card, on the dashboard and in
  Operations.
- Checkout says **Test mode: documents are not sent to DIAN**, and warns a site
  that has no valid range. Imported fiscal profiles now write their range to the
  new table and check the NIT verification digit.

### A desktop server that recovers

- If the built-in server stops, the app restarts it, up to three times in ten
  minutes. It never restarts after database corruption or a key failure.
- While it restarts, the till shows **Reconnecting to the server** and charging
  is disabled. Afterwards it says **The system recovered. Check the last sale.**
  Operations shows the number of server restarts in the last seven days.
- A sale whose answer was lost in the crash can be retried at once, and it is
  never recorded twice.
- The server has a memory cap and the app watches its memory, so a leak ends in
  a restart instead of a frozen till. The limits are provisional until they are
  measured on store hardware.
- Backups are built on a separate thread. On a test database with 120,000 rows,
  the longest pause of the window went from 64 ms to 10 ms. A scheduled backup
  waits for two minutes without sales, at most two hours, and checks free disk
  space before it starts.

### Fixes

- The cart and the product search show and check the stock of the active site.
- New products put their opening stock at the active site.
- Receipt amounts shared without a printer use the decimals of the sale
  currency.
- The DIAN reminders at checkout say what emission will actually do.
- The demo data uses NITs with valid verification digits.

## Before you use it

- This release adds two database migrations. They run on their own when the app
  starts.
- The Colombian fiscal pack is still in test mode. A registered range numbers
  documents in Puntovivo, but nothing is sent to DIAN.
- Windows builds are not code-signed yet, so a downloaded update waits for
  **Restart to install update** in the tray menu instead of installing on quit.
- Production sale is still gated by fiscal certification and physical-hardware
  validation, as the
  [project status](https://github.com/johnny4young/puntovivo/blob/main/docs/PROJECT-STATUS.md)
  describes.
