# Puntovivo v1.15.0 — accountable AI spend, reviewed payment proposals and steadier checkout

## Why this release matters

v1.15.0 changes how Puntovivo spends money on AI providers and how it lets AI
touch money records. Every paid AI call now takes a tenant-wide budget hold
before it reaches a provider, and settles afterwards at a known, estimated,
not-incurred or unknown cost. An unknown cost stays visible until an
administrator reconciles it. AI payment reconciliation suggestions now wait for
a person to accept them, and invoice OCR creates a purchase draft only from
net costs an operator has reviewed.

The release also carries checkout, inventory and setup corrections that make
everyday screens easier to trust, plus a sign-in hardening and dependency
security floors.

## What changed

### AI spend and review

- Generic completions, Copilot chat, voice transcription, legacy invoice vision
  and Textract invoice OCR reserve the tenant's monthly AI budget before
  dispatch. Only one paid call per tenant is in flight at a time.
- A call that may have been billed but returned no usable usage keeps an
  unknown-cost hold. Administrators see the blocked state in the AI settings
  card and can reconcile it with an audited cost and note. Provider answers
  that prove nothing was billed, such as a rate limit, a rejected credential or
  a connection that never opened, release the hold automatically.
- Closing the browser before a call is admitted cancels it without creating a
  hold. A call already sent to the provider runs to its deadline and records
  its cost instead of turning into an unknown liability.
- Copilot site quotas and the invoice OCR quota are rechecked inside the same
  admission, so two simultaneous requests cannot both pass a nearly used quota.
- Invoice PDFs are checked for a single page before a paid Textract request.
- Provider error text no longer reaches the client; operators get a bounded,
  privacy-safe summary in the server log.
- Copilot can show verified results only: the executed SQL, row count, table
  and chart without a generated conclusion. Known identities in its analytics
  snapshot are replaced with request-local pseudonyms.

### Payments and purchases

- AI payment reconciliation proposals require human review, and that review
  survives statement corrections. Provider news stays visible behind a
  proposal.
- Invoice OCR confirmation is atomic and idempotent: the purchase draft, its
  document number, audit entry and sync record are written together, and a
  retried confirmation returns the same draft. Operators confirm each line's
  net cost before the purchase is created.

### Checkout, inventory and setup

- The mobile cart and checkout stay inside narrow screens, including large
  money amounts. Checkout totals, cash suggestions and shortcut labels are
  clearer, and toasts no longer cover the screen on phones.
- Expiry radar and lot selection refresh on screens left open across midnight,
  and a lot that expires while the execute dialog is open is dropped from the
  draft instead of blocking the submit.
- The expiry discount editor is back under Company settings. The setup guide
  counts all six areas and refreshes after site changes.
- Dashboard reporting follows the tenant's business calendar. Unsupported
  tenant timezones show the existing repair guidance.

### Sign-in, sync and dependencies

- CSRF proofs are bound to the live refresh-token family, and the desktop Hub
  session keeps its refresh secret in the Electron main process.
- Local sync queue rows are processed atomically, and retries are fenced to
  the attempt that was observed.
- Dependency security floors were raised, including fast-jwt 6.3.4 for the
  expired-token cache advisory, and the unused raw database IPC bridge was
  removed from the desktop app.

## Before you use it

- These notes describe the proposed v1.15.0. Until the release is published,
  no installer exists for it.
- Installed registers are offered the update through the staged rollout. The
  update adds database migrations for AI payment proposals, Copilot site
  scope, AI budget reservations and invoice confirmation idempotency; let a
  register finish its shift before restarting into it.
- AI provider invoices and quotas remain authoritative. The local budget is a
  conservative admission control, not an exact invoice cap: a single call can
  exceed the remaining amount, and an unknown cost blocks further paid AI calls
  for the tenant until it is reconciled.
- macOS verifies an update's code signature and can install it on quit. Windows
  and Linux download the update but install it only when someone restarts into
  it from the app. The Windows installer is not code-signed.
- Production sale is still gated by fiscal certification and physical-hardware
  validation, as the
  [project status](https://github.com/johnny4young/puntovivo/blob/main/docs/PROJECT-STATUS.md)
  describes.
