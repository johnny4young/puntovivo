# Puntovivo v1.11.0 — services, flexible taxes and records that resist tampering

## Why this release matters

v1.11.0 widens what a store can sell and account for, and makes the records
behind those sales harder to change without anyone noticing. A store can now
sell services that never touch inventory, choose whether its prices include
tax, charge Colombia's consumption tax (INC) next to VAT (IVA), and give
wholesale customers their own price levels. Behind the counter, the audit log
became a verifiable chain, backups became authenticated bundles, and the
accountant gets files that import into their tools.

It is also the first release on the Electron 43 desktop runtime. It was the
last release before v1.14.2 whose desktop builds all published and whose
update reached installed registers automatically.

## What changed

### Selling and pricing

- Service items such as labor, delivery or a haircut sell without stock checks
  or inventory movements, and a cart that mixes services and products keeps
  physical stock correct.
- Each company chooses whether catalog prices include tax. INC is a tax kind
  next to IVA, frozen on every sale line and kept separate on fiscal document
  lines.
- Customers can carry one of three price levels, so a wholesale customer buying
  at their level is not flagged as a manual price override.
- Units carry international unit codes that travel to fiscal documents.
- The touch register accepts barcode scanner input, and twelve keyboard
  shortcuts for navigation and the register work as the shortcut sheet shows.
- Setup asks what kind of business you run. Table service moved into its own
  dine-in module, so a quick-service counter no longer sees tables.

### Records you can trust

- Audit entries form a chain per store. Administrators can verify its integrity
  from the audit page, and the chain is anchored with a secret that stays in
  the operating system keychain of that installation.
- Administrators can rotate the key of the encrypted database from the backups
  card.
- Backups are authenticated: changing, removing or adding any part of a bundle
  fails verification. An optional recovery passphrase lets another device
  restore a manual backup without typing the raw key.
- Every reveal of the backup key leaves an audit entry, and the key is withheld
  when that entry cannot be written.

### For the accountant and the owner

- Completed sales of a period export as a Siigo Nube sales invoice template,
  as balanced journal entries, or as one row per line. The export is blocked,
  not just warned, when the period is incomplete or a sale is unreconciled.
- An opt-in, read-only live view for managers and administrators, Seguimiento
  en vivo, follows the day's sales from a phone.

### Desktop

- The desktop app runs on Electron 43.4.1.
- On Windows and Linux a downloaded update waits until someone restarts into it
  from the app, because nothing verifies those packages yet. macOS keeps
  installing verified updates when the app quits.
- Fonts ship inside the app, so a register without internet never fetches them.
- The app starts in the store's language instead of switching after it appears.

## Before you use it

- These notes were written on 2026-09-13, after v1.14.2, to replace the
  generated notes this release was published with. For a desktop installation,
  use v1.14.2 or later.
- This release adds seven database migrations. Take an encrypted backup before
  upgrading an existing installation.
- Accountant exports leave company-specific vendor codes blank on purpose.
  Confirm the files with your accountant's importer before relying on them.
- Production sale is still gated by fiscal certification and physical-hardware
  validation, as the
  [project status](https://github.com/johnny4young/puntovivo/blob/main/docs/PROJECT-STATUS.md)
  describes.
