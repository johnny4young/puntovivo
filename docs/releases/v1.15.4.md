# Puntovivo v1.15.4 — A catalog you can browse and trust

## Why this release matters

v1.15.4 is about the product catalog and the dialogs around it. The products
page only ever loaded the first 50 products, so a larger catalog could not be
browsed without typing a search. Deactivating a product could leave it inactive
without telling the other devices, and an edit form opened before the
deactivation could quietly turn the product back on. Error messages raised
while a confirmation was open were not read out by screen readers, and keyboard
users lost their place when a confirmation was cancelled or still saving.

This release makes the catalog browsable page by page, makes catalog changes
and their sync records succeed or fail together, and keeps errors and keyboard
focus where the operator is working. It also moves every dependency with a
published security advisory to its patched release.

## What changed

### Browsing the catalog

- The products page pages through the whole catalog, 50 products at a time,
  with a stable order, so no product appears on two pages or on none.
- A search returns to the first page, and a result that fits on one page shows
  how many products matched.
- The product editor only loads when it is opened, so browsing the list and the
  details drawer stays lighter.

### Catalog changes that stay consistent

- Deactivating a product, and creating, editing or deleting a category,
  provider or unit, saves the change and its sync record together. A failure
  leaves both untouched instead of a change that never reaches other devices.
- Deactivation advances the product version, so an edit form opened before it
  can no longer reactivate the product by accident. Repeating a deactivation
  changes nothing.
- Renaming a category, provider, location, VAT rate or unit refreshes every
  screen that shows that name, including products, inventory, purchases,
  orders, quotations, sales and promotions.

### Errors and keyboard focus

- An error raised while a dialog is open appears inside that dialog, where
  screen readers announce it and its buttons can be reached.
- Cancelling a product deactivation returns focus to the row action that opened
  it, or to the search field when that row is gone.
- A confirmation keeps focus on its button while the request runs, and cannot
  be closed halfway through.
- A failed save in several settings screens no longer leaves an unhandled
  error behind; the screen keeps what you typed so you can retry.

### Dependencies

- Electron 43.8.3, Vite, Vitest, Fastify, Zod, sanitize-html, DOMPurify and
  several transitive HTTP and URL libraries move to their patched releases. The
  full dependency audit reports no known vulnerabilities.

## Before you use it

- This update has no database migration and no configuration change.
- Physical printer qualification and fiscal certification are still separate
  requirements. Production sale remains gated as the
  [project status](https://github.com/johnny4young/puntovivo/blob/main/docs/PROJECT-STATUS.md)
  describes.
- Installed registers are offered the update through the staged rollout; let a
  register finish its shift before restarting into it.
